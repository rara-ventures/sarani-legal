Privacy Policy

Effective 12 November 2026 · Rara Ventures LLC

The short version: Sarani does not collect, transmit or sell your personal data. There is no account, no sync, no server and no analytics. Your journal — text, photos, voice notes, video, records and mirrored health moods — is encrypted on your iPhone and stays there.

1. Who we are

Sarani is made by Rara Ventures LLC (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what the app does with information. Questions: raraventuresllc@gmail.com.

2. Information we do not collect

We do not operate a server for the journal and we do not receive your entries, media, records, health data, device identifier, usage history or crash logs. The app has no login, no user profile, no advertising, no tracking and no in-app purchase.

We do not use a third-party analytics or crash-reporting service. If that changes in a future version, this policy and the App Store privacy label will be updated before the change ships.

3. What is stored on your device

Everything you put into Sarani is stored locally and encrypted:

  • Journal entries. The text of each bubble, the day it belongs to, and any structure you confirm.
  • Attachments. Photos you add, voice notes you record, transcripts made from those notes, and the still frames and words kept from videos. Video files themselves are saved to your photo library, not to the journal.
  • Records and tables. Meals, purchases, watches, people, places and any types or collections you create.
  • Mood mirror. If you turn on the Health connection, State of Mind samples you logged in Apple Health are copied into your encrypted journal. Sarani only reads them; it never writes to Apple Health.
  • Settings. Theme, connection switches and other non-sensitive preferences. Secrets are never stored in plain preferences.

The journal database is encrypted with SQLCipher. Attachment files are encrypted separately. The keys are held in the iOS Keychain with this-device-only protection, so they do not travel in iCloud backups and are not available to us.

4. Permissions

Sarani asks only when a feature needs it, and you can change every permission in iOS Settings:

  • Microphone. Used to record voice notes. Audio is encrypted on the device.
  • Speech Recognition. Used to transcribe voice notes. Recognition runs on the device, and the audio is not sent to a transcription service.
  • Photos. Used when you choose a photo to add, and to save a video you recorded to your photo library. Sarani does not browse or upload your library.
  • Camera. Used only when you take a photo or record a video from inside the app.
  • Face ID. Used to unlock the journal. iOS performs the match; Sarani receives only success or failure and never sees your biometric data.
  • Health. Used only if you turn on the Health connection, and only to read State of Mind samples. Sarani does not read steps, sleep, workouts, medical records or any other health category.
  • Journaling Suggestions. Used only when you open Apple’s suggestion picker and choose something to add. Apple controls what the picker offers.

Sarani does not request access to your contacts, calendars or location. Your words about people and places are matched against names you have already used in the journal, not against your address book or GPS.

5. Optional connections that use the internet

The journal itself never needs the network. Two optional features do, and both talk directly from your device to the service you chose — we do not proxy the request or receive a copy:

  • Simkl. If you connect a Simkl account for films and television, the app exchanges a PIN with Simkl and stores the resulting token locally. Requests then go to Simkl’s API under your account. Simkl’s own privacy policy governs that data.
  • Link previews. If you share a web page into Sarani, the app requests that page to read its title and preview image. The request goes to the site that hosts the page, not to us.

When you use the iOS share sheet to send something out of Sarani, the destination you pick receives it. That is your choice and is outside Sarani’s encrypted storage.

6. Export and sharing

Sarani can export journal content at your request. An export is a plaintext copy, because its purpose is to be read elsewhere. The app hands it to the iOS share sheet and does not keep a second copy. Once exported, the file is protected by whatever destination you chose, not by Sarani.

7. Device backups

If you back up your iPhone to iCloud or a computer, iOS may include Sarani’s encrypted database and attachments in that backup. The encryption keys use this-device-only Keychain protection and are not included, and we never receive the backup. A backup restore may therefore contain ciphertext that cannot be opened on a different device; keep an export if you need a portable copy.

8. Retention and deletion

Your journal stays on your device until you delete it. Deleting the app removes its database and attachments from the device. iOS keeps Keychain items after an app is uninstalled, so a wrapped key may remain; it cannot read the deleted database and is removed when the device is erased.

There is no server copy to delete and no account to close. If you have exported a copy, delete it from wherever you saved it.

9. Children

Sarani is a general-purpose journal and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13, because we do not collect information at all.

10. Your choices

You can turn off any connection in Settings, decline any permission in iOS, and delete the app to remove its data. Because the journal is local, we cannot recover it for you if the device is lost or the app is deleted — export a copy if you need one.

11. Changes

If this policy changes, we will publish the new version on this page and update the effective date. Material changes will also be described in the app’s release notes.
