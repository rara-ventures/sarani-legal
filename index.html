A journal shaped like a conversation.

Each entry is a bubble. Each day is a thread. Sarani is private by design: your words, photos, voice notes and video are encrypted on your iPhone, and nothing is sent to us.

Write it the way you would say it

No forms, no notebooks, no blank page. The composer is a message field. A day is a thread you can scroll back through, and it ends at 4:00 AM so a late night still belongs to the evening it started in.

Type a line that records something and Sarani offers to file it as a record — a meal, a purchase, something you watched, a person or a place. The entry stays exactly as you wrote it. The card underneath is an offer, never an edit.

More than a feed

Add a photo, record a voice note transcribed on the device, or keep a video's words and still frame.

Tables that build themselves

Meals, purchases and watches become rows you can sort, filter and compare, with a page for every person and place.

Search that remembers

Full-text search over everything you have written, with the day and the thread always in reach.

Optional connections

Mirror Apple Health State of Mind, pull in Journaling Suggestions, or connect Simkl for films and TV.

Private by default

The journal is a SQLCipher database encrypted with keys held in the iOS Keychain. There is no account, no sync, no server and no analytics. We cannot read your journal, and we do not want to. Read the privacy policy for exactly what stays on the device and what happens when you choose to connect a service.

Requirements

iPhone or iPad running iOS 17 or later. Voice transcription, Journaling Suggestions and Health mood mirroring use Apple frameworks and may need a newer iOS version. There is no Android version at launch.
