Getting started

Sarani opens on today’s thread. Tap the message field to write. Each line becomes a bubble, and a line that records something — a meal, a purchase, something you watched, a person or a place — is offered as a record underneath it. Confirm it to file it, or ignore it and the prose stays prose.

The Library holds the days, the tables and the pages for people and places. Settings holds appearance, Face ID, export and the optional connections.

Common questions

Where is my journal stored?

On your iPhone, in an encrypted database. The keys live in the iOS Keychain with this-device-only protection. We do not have a copy and cannot read it.

Does Sarani sync between devices?

Not yet. There is no account, no cloud and no sync. A journal belongs to the device that wrote it. To move one, use Settings → Export journal and keep the exported file somewhere safe.

How do I back up my journal?

Use Settings → Export journal. An export is a plaintext copy, so store it somewhere you trust. Device backups may include the encrypted database, but the separate encryption keys are this-device-only and are not backed up, so a backup is not a reliable way to move a journal to a new phone.

I deleted the app. Can you restore my journal?

No. There is no server copy and no account. Deleting the app deletes the journal. If you have an export, it can be imported into a fresh install.

Why does Sarani ask for Face ID?

To unlock the journal. iOS performs the match and Sarani only learns whether it succeeded. Face ID is currently always on; you can turn it off in iOS Settings if you prefer the device passcode.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the app. That removes the journal database and attachments from the device. iOS may retain a wrapped key in the Keychain, but it cannot read the deleted database. Erasing the device removes it.

Voice notes are not transcribing

Check that Microphone and Speech Recognition are allowed in iOS Settings → Sarani. Transcription runs on the device and needs a supported language pack; if the language is not available, the recording is still saved and you can play it back.

The Health connection is missing or empty

State of Mind is available on iOS 18 and later, and only when you have logged moods in Apple Health. Open the Health connection in Settings, grant read access, then choose Sync now. Sarani never writes to Health.

Journaling Suggestions do not appear

Suggestions come from Apple and are not available in every region or on every device. The rest of the journal, including the on-device parser, works without them.

How do I save a link or page into Sarani?

Use the iOS share sheet from Safari or another app and choose Sarani. The share extension stores the link in the app group and opens the app. One link or one piece of text at a time.

How do I connect Simkl?

Open Settings → Simkl, start the PIN flow, and enter the code on Simkl’s site. The token is stored locally. Disconnect at any time from the same screen.

Does Sarani work on iPad?

Sarani 1.0 is an iPhone app. It can run on an iPad in iPhone compatibility mode, but the layout is designed for a phone.

Which iOS versions are supported?

iOS 17 and later. Health mood mirroring needs iOS 18 or later, and Apple’s Foundation Models parser needs iOS 26 or later. On older versions Sarani uses its own deterministic parser, which handles the common record lines without a model.
